## Platform

web

## Users
US car buyers shopping for a new or used car who don't want to spend a weekend on listing sites, review forums, dealer calls and the finance office. They arrive curious about who CarXprt is and whether it can be trusted with a big purchase.

## Product Purpose
CarXprt is an AI car marketplace. A buyer describes what they want in plain language (typed or by voice), browses matching dealer inventory in the Discovery Hub, saves cars to a Garage, and lets the Xprt Negotiator ask dealers for their best out-the-door price. The buyer approves the final deal. The About us page explains that story and sends people to start a search.

## Positioning
The negotiator works for the buyer, not the dealer: it talks to one dealer at a time and works the out-the-door price down, the buyer's contact details stay private on Plus (Info: Private), and the full out-the-door price is shown before signing. The main edge over listing sites: the whole thing can be done by talking to the AI. (No price confidence score; never "dealers compete".)

## Operating Context
Flow in the product: chat bar with ZIP, filters and suggested searches → Discovery Hub results (filter chips, car cards with price, location, fuel, mileage) → deal page with Chat / Quotes & Docs / Negotiator tabs and an "Xprt Negotiator" toggle → buyer approval.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Search by chat or voice ("Speak to CarXprt"), ZIP-based, multi-dealer inventory.
- Garage for saved cars; side-by-side compare.
- AI negotiator and human expert on paid plans (see compare-sources.md).
- Time claim in use (22 Oct 2026): "~10 hrs" saved, 75% less active time (Proof and the walkthrough finale): about 14 active hours today against about 3½ with CarXprt. Today is from Cox Automotive 2025 averages and typical dealer timelines; CarXprt is from customer journeys. Needs legal confirmation.

## Brand Commitments
- Figma design system is binding: Helvetica Neue only; ink #1A1A1A, lime #E7F852, ground #F5F5F7, white surfaces; dark mode supported. Figma sizes are not a system.
- No eyebrow labels, no 01–04 numbering, no filler words ("Example", "Illustrative"), no monthly-payment content.
- Not affiliated with any car brand: no manufacturer badges in imagery.

## Product Principles
1. The buyer stays in control: CarXprt proposes, the buyer approves.
2. Show the whole price before the signature.
3. Privacy by default: dealers see a deal ID, not the buyer's phone number.
4. Prove with the product's real screens, never with invented numbers.
